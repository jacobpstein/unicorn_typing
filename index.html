For grown-ups 👋

Rainbow Quest is a typing adventure. She travels an overworld map, exploring and unlocking themed worlds, collecting coins, and beating a friendly "boss" word at the end of each world. Every correct key moves her hero forward and races a unicorn to the flag.

It adapts automatically: word difficulty, the unicorn's speed, and how many hints show all adjust from her accuracy and first-try rate — easing back when she struggles, pushing when she's winning. Losing a race never blocks her; it just eases the next try.

Good habits: keys are color-coded by finger, the home row (with the F & J bumps) is outlined, and the hands guide shows which finger to use.
